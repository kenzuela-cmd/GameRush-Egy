Got Questions?
We've Got Answers.

Everything you need to know before starting your next gaming adventure.

What is the difference between Primary and Secondary accounts?

Primary Account: Play your purchased game or use PS Plus directly from your personal PlayStation account without requiring a constant internet connection. Do not log into the purchased account again after activation.


Secondary Account: Play directly from the provided account with a constant internet connection. PS4 users can switch to their personal account. Changing account credentials, adding security settings or activating the account as Primary is not permitted.


Full Account: Receive the complete account credentials. The account can be used on up to three consoles, with a combination of PS4 and PS5 devices.


GameRush provides activation support for all account types.

How do I order a game?

Ordering from GameRush is quick and easy!


  1. Browse our game collection.
  2. Choose your preferred game and console.
  3. Select your preferred account type.
  4. Contact us through WhatsApp or Messenger.
  5. Confirm your order and complete the payment.

Once your payment is confirmed, your account will be delivered within 10 minutes.

What payment methods are available?

We offer several convenient payment methods:


  • Vodafone Cash
  • InstaPay
  • Telda
  • Binance (for international payments)

Contact our team through WhatsApp or Messenger to receive the payment details and confirm your order.

How will I receive my game after payment?

Once your payment has been confirmed, your account will be delivered within 10 minutes.


Primary and Secondary Accounts: Your account will be provided through a QR code for activation on your PlayStation console.


Full Accounts: You will receive the complete account credentials.


Both provided accounts and personal account options are available. Our team will guide you through the activation process and provide assistance whenever needed.
